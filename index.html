Aditi, I'm sorry.

For not being him — and for taking this long to bring him back.

12 balloons popped· 4 hot ones, handled· forgiven (probation: forever)

Two years ago, you met a guy who listened. Who was patient. Who made your long days lighter instead of asking you to carry his too. I know exactly when I stopped being him — when I started treating your patience like a guarantee instead of the gift it is.

You spend 13-hour shifts caring for everyone who can't ask for help. You shouldn't have to come home and manage me too. Yet I let the same mistake put that weight on you, again and again. Your eyes are the most beautiful thing I have ever seen, and I keep being the reason they're sad. That's the part I can't joke my way out of.

So I'm not promising you a stranger. I'm promising to bring back the guy you met two years ago — the one who paused before he reacted, who listened before he answered, who made you feel at ease. Him, consistently. Not just when he's scared of losing you.

No more explosions. No more making you carry it. Just him, showing up — boringly, reliably, every day. Aditi, I'm sorry it took me this long to come back.
